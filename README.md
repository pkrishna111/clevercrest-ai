# CleverCrest AI

> Multi-Tenant Enterprise Knowledge Intelligence Platform with secure RAG-powered AI assistance.

CleverCrest AI is a modular enterprise knowledge intelligence platform designed to help organizations manage controlled internal knowledge and provide permission-aware AI assistance over approved organizational information.

The AI Assistant is a capability of the platform, not the entire product.

---

## Project Status

**Current Version:** 1.0 MVP  
**Development Stage:** Platform Foundation / Authentication

The project is being developed incrementally, following an MVP-first approach.

The primary goal is to build a secure, reliable and demonstrable Version 1.0 before adding advanced future capabilities.

---

## Product Vision

CleverCrest combines organizational knowledge management, governance, access control and Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG).
